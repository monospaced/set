`effect.blend.overprint` is the blend mode for overlapping inks: `multiply` on light, `screen` on dark, so a crossing darkens on a light ground and brightens on a dark one. It follows the theme like the colour tokens, including the OS scheme and locked content themes. Decorative only; blended colours aren't contrast-safe.
