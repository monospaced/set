Spec text that says what to do, not just what happens. `Nav`'s `collapsible` says a collapsible nav must be the first or last item in its row, so the expander sits at the row's edge, and `expanderPosition` names that edge (`start` for the first item, `end` for the last) and says it is ignored unless `collapsible` is set. `Avatar`'s `color` says to leave it unset for a named subject, so the color derives from the name, and that `neutral` is the fallback for an avatar with no name.
