Dither, a new component that lays a tone of ordered dither behind content. `density` from 0 to 100 snaps to 17 levels of a 4×4 Bayer matrix in square dots; `ramp` fades from solid to empty toward an end, in 17 bands that share one tile phase so the pattern runs unbroken at any length. `size` sets the cell width from 3 to 48px and `tone` the ink, as on Pattern. Pattern is unchanged: its tiles are the brand's staggered lattice in 1:2 character cells, a different texture from a dither.
